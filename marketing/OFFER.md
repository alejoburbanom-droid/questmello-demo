# Offer hypothesis — not yet validated

**Product concept:** personalized birthday treasure hunt played at home with five stages and printable clues. Intended purchaser is a parent or other adult party organizer.

**Value proposition:** prepare an original birthday adventure with common household hiding spots and one mobile device, without buying equipment.

**Price test later:** $19 versus $24 for premium adventure packs. Current preview remains **free** and cannot accept orders.

**Primary metric:** paid orders from qualified visitors once secure checkout exists. Before then: adult organizer usability results, kit print attempts and expressed purchase intent (not misrepresented as sales).

**Risks:** many free and low-priced alternatives, weak organic distribution, puzzle quality, brand clearance, privacy/child-safety obligations and seasonality. Do not collect children's personal data for analytics.
